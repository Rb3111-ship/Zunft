Zunft: pricing proposal

Research and proposal, 14 October 2026. All prices are net (without 19 % VAT) unless marked gross. Market prices come from comparison sites and vendor pages found today; please check the vendors' own pages before you rely on a number.
Short answer Charge about 15 € per month for a solo craftsman, 39 € for a team of up to 5 and 69 € for up to 10, with two months free when paid yearly. That sits between simple invoicing tools (8–33 €) and full craft suites (35–80 €), while Zunft offers what invoicing tools do not: speaking a job instead of typing it, a calendar and time tracking. The costs per customer are small (about 1–2 € per person per month), so the margin is high; the real work is winning customers, not covering costs.

Break-even: fixed costs of about 30 € per month are covered by 3 solo customers. Illustration (not a forecast): 50 Solo + 20 Team + 5 Betrieb on monthly billing is about 1,870 € revenue and 260 € costs, so about 1,600 € per month before your own taxes, support time and development.

4. Open points and risks

Check before you decide
  • Google Play billing: if subscriptions are sold inside the app, Google takes 15–30 %, which would turn Solo into about 12.70 €. Selling on our website and only reading the status in the app avoids this, but the rules are changing: read Google's current policy for your case first.
  • VAT: prices are net because the customers are businesses. Whether you charge VAT depends on your own status (Kleinunternehmer or not): a question for your tax advisor.
  • Price sensitivity: self-employed craftsmen compare against 8–13 € invoicing tools. Solo at 14.90 is only credible if the voice feature saves real time. The pilot will tell: if people hesitate, test 9.90 as the regular Solo price; if nobody hesitates, 19.90 is possible.
  • Storage and AI growth: heavy users of receipts and recordings cost more; the 1 GB limit and the 90-day recording retention keep that in check.
  • Legal: terms of service, cancellation rules (monthly vs yearly), and what happens to data after cancelling need the lawyer (To-Do, task 3).
