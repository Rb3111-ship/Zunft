1. What Zunft is

Zunft is an app for small German craft businesses (Handwerk): an electrician who works alone, up to a firm of about ten people. It replaces the evening of paperwork. The craftsman talks into the phone on site, and the app turns that into a correct, numbered invoice or quote.

1. Speak"Steckdose im Keller, zwei Stunden, Material vierzig Euro"
→
2. UnderstandSpeech to text, then an AI sorts it into positions
→
3. CheckThe craftsman reviews and corrects every position
→
4. InvoiceNumbered PDF (DIN 5008), optionally an e-invoice

The problems it solves

  • Paperwork after work. Positions are created from speech, text or a photographed receipt. The craftsman only checks.
  • Tax rules for private customers (§ 35a EStG). A private customer can deduct labour costs, but not material. The invoice therefore shows labour and material apart and prints the amounts the customer needs for the tax return.
  • Several people, one invoice. Everything recorded on a job by anyone (voice, receipts, tracked time) is collected into one invoice.
  • Bad reception on site. The app works offline and sends everything when the connection is back.
  • Legal safety. Issued invoices can never be changed (only cancelled with a Stornorechnung), numbers have no gaps, and amounts are calculated by program code, never by the AI.

Who uses it

RoleWhoWhat they can do
Inhaber (owner)The boss, or a self-employed person aloneEverything, including team, plan and settings

One account belongs to one person and one business. Removing someone never deletes their account; they just lose access to the business data.

2. The big picture

Zunft has three parts: the app on the phone, the cloud that keeps the data for all phones of a business, and two AI services that understand speech and text.

Phone appFlutter (Dart). Own small database on the phone. Works offline.
↔
Cloud: SupabaseDatabase (Postgres), file storage, login, small server programs (Edge Functions), nightly jobs. Region: EU (Ireland).
↔
AI servicesDeepgram (speech to text) and Google Gemini (understanding text and photos). Called only by our cloud, never by the phone.

The path of one voice note

  1. The craftsman picks a job and records up to 3 minutes. The app plays it back so he can check it before sending ("Anhören / Senden / Löschen").
  2. The sound file is saved on the phone first (so nothing is lost without network) and queued.
  3. When online, the app uploads the file to private cloud storage and calls the Edge Function process-capture with the job id.
  4. The function checks who is calling (login) and that the job belongs to their business, downloads the file, and asks Deepgram for the German text.
  5. The text goes to Gemini with strict instructions and a fixed answer format: positions with type (labour, material, flat fee), quantity, unit, price. The text is treated as data, not as commands.
  6. Our code validates the answer and calculates totals (integer cents). Prices the AI had to guess are marked "Preis prüfen".
  7. The result is stored as a note on the job. The customer always comes from the job, never from what the AI heard.
  8. The phone receives the note; the craftsman reviews all notes of the job together and issues the invoice.
Why the AI never calculatesA language model can make arithmetic mistakes. So it only copies what was said or printed. Net, VAT, totals, net-from-gross conversion and cross-checks against printed receipt totals are done by normal program code that is tested.

Offline and sync

The phone keeps its own copy of customers, jobs, appointments, time entries and issued documents (database Drift/SQLite). Every change is written locally first and put in an outbox. When the network is there, the outbox is sent to the cloud, in an order that respects links (customer before job, job before appointment). Then the phone pulls what others changed. If two people change the same thing, the last write wins. The only things that need internet: issuing a document (the number comes from the server), AI processing, and an employee marking an appointment done.

5. What the app does

5.1 Jobs (Aufträge)

Two tabs: Offen (what still needs work: in-progress first) and Erledigt (finished and billed; finished-but-not-billed first, because that is money to collect). A job moves to Erledigt when it is invoiced or finished by hand; a closed job accepts no new notes until the boss reopens it. The status changes to "in progress" by itself with the first note. The boss assigns workers to a job; a worker only sees jobs assigned to him.

5.2 Customers

  • Created by hand or via an invoice; the customer on an invoice comes from the job.
  • Duplicates: a name alone is never treated as a duplicate (there are many Müllers). A warning appears when the same phone or e-mail exists under another name, or the same name at the same address. Same name at a different address is a different person.
  • Merging (boss and office): side-by-side comparison; jobs and appointments move to the customer you keep; missing details are filled in and different details are written into the notes. The merged record stays hidden because issued documents still point at it. There is no undo button.
  • A customer who has a quote or invoice cannot be deleted (legal retention).

5.3 Capturing work

Voice and text

Up to 3 minutes per voice note, or type instead. Listen before sending. The recording can be replayed later with every number highlighted. Recordings are kept as raw material only: they are deleted a set number of days after the job closes (default 90, the business may shorten it). The text and positions stay.

Receipts (Belege)

For material bought for a customer: scan with the document scanner, choose a photo, or upload a PDF. The AI copies printed values; code does the arithmetic:

  • Net or gross is decided from the printed numbers: subtotal + VAT = total means net prices; lines add up to the total means gross prices (divided by 1.19 in code). The AI's own guess does not decide.
  • Warnings for: sum differs from printed total, deposit (Pfand), unreadable lines, assumed VAT rate.
  • Same receipt twice: same file (checksum) or same receipt number + date + total (or supplier + date + total without a number). It only warns; the boss confirms.
  • The material goes onto the job at purchase price plus the business's optional markup (receipt itself keeps the real cost). Article numbers (EAN) are stripped from invoice text.
  • Archive "Einkaufsbelege": all receipts with status (open / billed / removed), search, and a ZIP export (photos per month, CSV lists) for the tax advisor.

5.4 Reviewing and invoicing

  • All open notes of a job become one draft. Positions are typed: labour (Arbeitsleistung), flat fee (counts as labour) and material. Every position can be edited, added or removed.
  • Positions the AI had to estimate are flagged "Preis prüfen" and must be confirmed before issuing.
  • Tracked working time can be billed: the boss chooses to replace spoken working time with tracked time, bill both, or not now (see 5.8).
  • Issuing is one server call: it checks the data, assigns the next number and stores the locked document in one step. If the connection drops, a retry returns the same document instead of a second number.
  • Required before an invoice: sender address and tax number or USt-IdNr; for amounts over 250 € the customer's address.
  • A quote (Angebot) is made the same way and has its own number series; it is never revenue.

5.5 Documents

  • PDF in DIN 5008 layout with the business logo, Leistungszeitraum, payment term, bank details, and for private customers the § 35a notice with labour and material amounts. A Kleinunternehmer (VAT rate 0 %) gets the § 19 UStG note.
  • Immutable: an issued document cannot be edited or deleted. A mistake is corrected with a Stornorechnung (negative amounts, own number ST-). The original is marked cancelled, its notes are released and the job reopens so the work can be billed again.
  • E-invoice (ZUGFeRD / Factur-X, EN 16931): the same PDF is a PDF/A-3 file with the machine-readable XML inside. Pre-selected for company customers; needs complete data (checked before a number is issued). A cancellation of an e-invoice is an e-invoice credit note. Validated with the open-source Mustang validator.
  • Sending: share sheet (WhatsApp, e-mail with the customer's address pre-filled). Status: issued → sent → paid (by hand).
  • Search by number, customer, status or amount; summary of open invoices.

5.6 Calendar (Termine)

  • Day and week view, week strip with dots, filter by person for the boss. Appointments belong to people, optionally to a job and a customer (address pre-filled).
  • Absences (holiday, sick), all-day, multi-day.
  • Double-booking warning: overlap in time and a shared person; you may still save.
  • A worker given an appointment for a job is automatically assigned to that job.
  • In an appointment: call the customer, open the route in the maps app, add to the phone calendar (.ics), send the customer a friendly confirmation text, mark done, cancel (boss).
  • Reminders on the phone (15 min to 2 h before), planned by the phone itself, so they work with the app closed and without internet.
  • Push messages (switch "Meldungen bei Terminen" per phone): when the boss gives a worker an appointment, moves or cancels it, takes the worker off it or deletes it, the worker's phone gets a notification even with the app closed. How: the database notices the change and calls the function notify-appointment, which looks up the worker's phones and sends the message through Google Firebase. The person who made the change is not notified. Dead phone addresses are removed automatically. Each person switches it on once, on their own phone; signing out switches it off, so a shared phone never shows someone else's messages.

5.7 Overview (Übersicht)

For boss and office: per month or year the plain sums of issued invoices (net, VAT per rate, gross, labour/material, paid/open, cancellations netted) and of purchase receipts, plus a CSV to share. It is an addition of stored records by invoice date, labelled "Zusammenstellung, keine Steuerberechnung". It does not compute VAT payable, profit or any tax figure.

5.8 Working time (Zeiterfassung)

  • Timer on a job (or from an appointment): a bar above the tabs shows what is running. One timer per person; starting another stops the first. A timer over 12 hours is flagged as forgotten.
  • By hand: "Zeit nachtragen" with start, end and break. The boss can add time for a worker; the entry is then marked "vom Betrieb eingetragen" so the worker sees who made it.
  • Weekly list per person (boss) or "Meine Zeiten" (worker; only his own).
  • On the invoice: one position "Arbeitszeit (Zeiterfassung)" at the business's hourly rate, flagged for a price check. The entries are then locked and not suggested again.
  • No location is recorded.
Does the timer drain the battery? What if the app is closed?No. The app does not run anything in the background. When you press start it stores a single fact: the start time. The clock you see is just "now minus start time", worked out whenever the screen is open (the one-second refresh only runs while the timer is visible). If the app is closed or the phone restarts, nothing is lost and nothing is consumed: the next time you open it, the elapsed time is calculated from the stored start time. Reminders use the phone's own alarm system for the same reason. If you prefer the simple way, "Zeit nachtragen" lets a worker enter only a start and an end time.
Boss entering time for a worker: is it legal?Not checked, and the app does not claim it. In practice the owner is the one responsible for working-time records and many firms enter time for staff, but the rules on recording working hours, on a worker's right to see and object, and on works council co-determination need a lawyer. The app is built to be transparent: the worker sees the entry in "Meine Zeiten" marked "vom Betrieb eingetragen". There is no change log yet. This is item 3.5 in the To-Do document.

5.9 Security and privacy in the app

  • Row-level security: the database itself refuses to show or change another business's data, and an employee only sees what is assigned to them. This is enforced in the database, not only in the app.
  • App lock (fingerprint, face or phone PIN) per phone, with a timeout; sign-out always works.
  • Storage limit per business (default 1 GB, set by us); uploads are refused when full, invoices are never blocked.
  • Recording retention (default 90 days after job closure), enforced nightly by the server.
  • The employee privacy notice in the app lists what is stored. It is a draft for a lawyer.
  • Secret keys (Deepgram, Gemini, cron secret) exist only on the server.

7. Decisions that stand

  • Gemini, not Claude, for parsing (cost). Deepgram stays for speech; no model change and no multi-language yet.
  • A flat fee (Anfahrt, Notdienst) counts as labour for § 35a; the advisor must confirm.
  • One business per account. Owner account deletion is a support process (invoices must be kept by law).
  • Payments are deferred: seat and storage limits are set by hand in SQL.
  • Deliberately not built: bank connection and payment matching, link to the customer's own cloud, quote-to-invoice conversion, own price list, any tax calculation (VAT payable, EÜR, Voranmeldung), DATEV export before the advisor says what is needed.
