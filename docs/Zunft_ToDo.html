1. Tax advisor meeting

Why: the invoice texts and calculations are proposals until an advisor confirms themTime: 1 hour

Zunft prints a § 35a notice, splits labour and material, issues cancellations, sells material onward with a markup and now creates e-invoices. A wrong sentence on thousands of invoices is a real risk. The advisor's answers decide whether any wording or calculation changes.

Prepare (30 minutes)

1
In the app, on your test business, create these examples and send them to yourself by e-mail as PDF: (a) an invoice for a private customer with labour, a flat fee and material; (b) an invoice for a company customer as an e-invoice; (c) a Stornorechnung of a test invoice; (d) a quote.
2
Export the receipt archive of one month: Belege → Einkaufsbelege → Exportieren (ZIP). Also share the monthly overview: Betrieb → Übersicht → Als Datei teilen (CSV).
3
Print Zunft_Steuerberater.pdf (in the docs folder). It lists about 20 questions with a box for the answer under each. Bring it, the printed examples and the ZIP on a USB stick or e-mailed.

At the meeting

4
Start with the questions that can change the product most: A1/A2 (flat fee as labour, the § 35a sentence), C1/C2 (Stornorechnung and paid invoices), E1/E2 (material bought for the customer, input VAT on till receipts), E3 (keeping receipts, throwing away the paper), W1 (e-invoice: ZUGFeRD enough or also XRechnung?), W6 (billing tracked hours).
5
Ask two extra questions that are not on the list: (a) does the advisor want the export in a particular format (for example DATEV)? (b) does the business need a written Verfahrensdokumentation (GoBD process description), and may I draft it from the Project Guide?
6
Write the answers in the boxes. If the advisor says something is wrong, do not argue; just note the exact words.

Afterwards

7
Photograph or type the answers and send them to me. I change the texts and calculations accordingly, add tests, and update the three documents.
Good to knowNo answer is needed to keep testing. But do not send a real invoice to a real customer from Zunft before this meeting has happened.
The Leitweg-ID (only for invoices to public authorities)You do not apply for it. It is the buyer's routing number: a public authority (city, state office, ministry, school administration) tells you its Leitweg-ID in the order, contract or its invoicing instructions (often on the authority's website under "Rechnungsstellung" or "E-Rechnung"). It looks like numbers separated by hyphens. You need it only for the pure-XML XRechnung format that public bodies require; private companies are served by the ZUGFeRD e-invoice Zunft already makes. Ask the advisor in question W1 whether you will have public customers at all. If you get your first one, send me the Leitweg-ID format they use and I add the field and the XRechnung export.

2. Data protection basics (you can do this yourself)

Why: customer names, addresses and voices go to outside servicesCost: freeTime: 2-3 hours

Where the data goes: the phone → Supabase (database and files; your project is in the EU, Ireland) → Deepgram (voice to text) and Google Gemini (understanding text and receipt photos). The craftsmen who use Zunft are the controllers of their customers' data; you are their processor, and Deepgram, Google and Supabase are your sub-processors. You need a signed data-processing agreement (AVV / DPA) with each of the three, and the AI settings must be right.

2.1 Supabase

1
Open supabase.com/dashboard, click your organisation name (top left) → Organization settings → Legal Documents (also reachable through the "Legal" or "DPA" entry). Read the DPA and use the button to sign or request it.
2
Save the signed PDF in a folder called Zunft-Vertraege on your computer and in cloud storage.
3
Check the region once: project zunft → Settings → General: it must say a EU region (it does: eu-west-1, Ireland). Do not create the production project anywhere else.

2.2 Google Gemini (the most important setting)

1
Open aistudio.google.com → API keys. Find the key that Zunft uses and look at which project it belongs to.
2
Click Set up billing for that project (it opens Google Cloud). As far as I know, Google's terms treat content sent on the free tier differently from the paid tier: on the free tier Google may use the content to improve its products; on the paid tier it does not. Real customer data must only go through a project with billing enabled. Please read Google's current "Gemini API Additional Terms" yourself, because these terms change.
3
In the Google Cloud console for that project: Billing → Budgets & alerts → create a monthly budget (for example 20 EUR) with e-mail alerts at 50 %, 90 % and 100 %.
4
Find and accept the Cloud Data Processing Addendum (Google Cloud Console → IAM & Admin → Privacy & Security or the "Legal" page of Google Cloud). Save the confirmation.

2.3 Deepgram

1
Open console.deepgram.com. Look in Settings / Project for any option about "model improvement" or data retention. Make sure you are not taking part in a programme that lets Deepgram keep or train on your audio. If you cannot find it, write to their support and ask in plain words: "Do you store or use our audio for training? Please send your DPA and your list of sub-processors."
2
Ask for the signed DPA and save it with the others. Ask where the audio is processed (several providers are in the USA; that needs a legal basis for international transfers, which the lawyer in task 3 checks).

2.4 Afterwards

5
Send me: "DPA signed with Supabase / Google / Deepgram: yes or no, and where the audio is processed". I put the right names into the privacy text and the list of service providers for the lawyer.
Until this is doneUse only invented data (like in the Test Plan) and your own voice. Do not record real customers' names or addresses.

5. Signing key and Google Play

Why: today the APK is signed with a debug key, which Google Play does not acceptCost: one-time Google feeTime: 1 hour + days of waiting
Critical: the key is irreplaceableThe upload key identifies your app. If you lose it or its passwords, updating the app becomes a long support case. Make two copies in different places (for example an encrypted USB stick and a password manager). Never put it in Git or send it by e-mail.

5.1 Create the key

1
Open a terminal (PowerShell) and run (all on one line; it asks for two passwords and some questions about your name; use real values): 
D:\Android\jdk17\bin\keytool.exe -genkey -v -keystore D:\secrets\zunft-upload.jks -keyalg RSA -keysize 2048 -validity 10000 -alias zunft-upload
Create the folder D:\secrets first. Write down: the file path, the store password, the key password, and the alias zunft-upload in your password manager.
2
Tell me: "key created at D:\secrets\zunft-upload.jks". Do not send me the passwords in the chat. I will prepare the build so that it reads the passwords from a local file (android/key.properties, excluded from Git) which you create yourself with four lines (storePassword, keyPassword, keyAlias, storeFile). I give you the exact content when you tell me.

11. iPhone version (optional)

Is the Apple developer programme free? No.
  • Apple Developer Program: about 99 USD per year (a company or an individual). Without it you cannot use TestFlight, cannot publish in the App Store and cannot use push messages.
  • A free Apple ID lets a developer install an app on his own iPhone from a Mac, but it expires after 7 days and cannot be shared with testers. That is only for tinkering.
  • You also need a Mac (or a paid cloud Mac / build service; some have a small free allowance).
  • Registering as a company needs a free D-U-N-S number and a website; this takes days to weeks. The fee is waived only for non-profits, schools and government bodies.
  • For comparison, Google Play is a one-time fee (about 25 USD).
Why: many craftsmen use iPhonesCost: Apple fee per yearNeeds: a Mac

iPhone apps can only be built on a Mac (or a cloud Mac service). The code is already written for both systems; what is missing is the build and Apple's paperwork.

1
Join the Apple Developer Program at developer.apple.com/programs (identity check; organisations need a D-U-N-S number, which takes extra time).
2
Get Mac access: your own Mac, a rented cloud Mac (for example MacinCloud) or a cloud build service for Flutter (for example Codemagic). Ask me which one fits; I prepare the project for it.
3
In App Store Connect create the app with the bundle ID I give you; upload via TestFlight to test with a few iPhones.
4
For push on iPhone: create an APNs key (Certificates, Identifiers & Profiles → Keys), upload it in Firebase → Project settings → Cloud Messaging → Apple app configuration (needs task 6).
5
Expect to answer Apple's review questions about privacy (same facts as the Play Data safety form) and to provide a demo account.
